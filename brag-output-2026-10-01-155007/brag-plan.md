# Brag Plan: Smart Canteen

## What is this app?
Smart Canteen is an AI-assisted pre-order and queue-management platform that lets customers order food before reaching the counter while staff manage preparation, pickup, stock, and analytics.

## The angle
A complete lunch-break workflow in one visible system: the customer orders, the kitchen receives a prioritized queue, the collection desk verifies a one-time token, and the manager sees the operation become measurable through analytics and AI forecasts.

## Hook
Open on the lunch rush problem: a packed counter and a sharp title card, then cut immediately to the Smart Canteen menu with available stock and pickup slots.

## Key moments
- Customer menu browsing with food images, categories, price, stock, prep-time filters, and AI recommendations.
- Cart checkout with quantity controls, special instructions, pickup slot selection, and digital token creation.
- Customer live-order tracker showing status progression and ETA.
- Kitchen dashboard showing priority queue, special instructions, status changes, and delay reporting.
- Collection verification using token or QR, followed by completed order state.
- Manager dashboard with KPIs, slot utilization, sales reports, waste risk, peak-time prediction, demand forecasting, prep recommendations, delay prediction, and smart recommendations.

## Outro
"From browse to pickup, every order has a place in the queue." End on Smart Canteen and the project capabilities: Pre-order. Queue. Prepare. Collect. Learn.

## User flow worth showing
Menu -> cart and pickup slot -> digital token -> kitchen queue -> ready notification/tracking -> token verification and collection -> manager analytics and AI operations cockpit.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: a confident product demo built around the lunch-break journey
- Interpretation: smooth transitions, readable labels, real UI surfaces, restrained motion, and clear operational proof.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 90 seconds

## Visual identity
- Background: #f8f9ff and white app surfaces
- Accent: orange-600 for customer actions, blue-600 for manager identity, emerald for healthy stock/status, slate-900 for operational panels
- Text: slate-900 and white on dark panels
- Display font: project headline font / sans fallback
- Body font: project sans-serif
- Strongest visual element: real menu cards, digital tokens, queue rows, and the dark manager AI operations cockpit.

## Share copy
Smart Canteen turns a lunch rush into a coordinated flow: pre-order, digital token, live kitchen queue, QR collection, and AI-powered canteen operations in one system.

## Audio direction
- Role: polished upbeat product bed with light interface accents
- Music: bundled happy-beats-business-moves track
- Music treatment: fade in under the hook, lift during the kitchen and analytics reveals, fade under the final title
- SFX posture: moderate and restrained; use clicks, short confirmation tones, queue ticks, and a soft completion hit
- Restraint rule: audio supports the workflow and never competes with readable UI labels.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The lunch rush — 0:00-0:08
Visual: dark slate title card with the problem: "Lunch break should not mean queue break." Transition into the Smart Canteen landing/menu screen.
Text: "Smart Canteen" and "Pre-order. Track. Collect."
Interaction: title resolves into the actual customer menu.

### Scene 2 — Browse the menu — 0:08-0:20
Visual: real menu grid with burger, fries, drinks, meals, images, prices, stock levels, category filters, availability, and prep-time controls.
Text: "See what is available before you reach the counter."
Interaction: search/filter and highlight one item, then show the AI recommendation rail.

### Scene 3 — Smart recommendations — 0:20-0:29
Visual: authenticated customer recommendation section with cards and Add to cart actions.
Text: "Recommendations based on available and popular choices."
Interaction: click Add to cart on a recommended item.

### Scene 4 — Cart and pickup slot — 0:29-0:40
Visual: cart page with quantities, special instructions, total, and pickup slot capacity.
Text: "Choose quantities. Add instructions. Reserve a 15-minute pickup slot."
Interaction: increase quantity, add instruction, select an available slot, confirm.

### Scene 5 — Token and live tracking — 0:40-0:51
Visual: order confirmation with token such as C-023, QR code, estimated ready time, and progress state.
Text: "One order. One digital token. Live status."
Interaction: progress from Placed to Accepted to Preparing.

### Scene 6 — Kitchen queue — 0:51-1:03
Visual: kitchen dashboard with prioritized orders, scheduled pickup urgency, item details, special instructions, ETA, and action buttons.
Text: "The kitchen sees what matters next."
Interaction: accept order, move to Preparing, demonstrate Report Delay changing the order to Delayed.

### Scene 7 — Ready and collection — 1:03-1:13
Visual: order reaches Ready, customer sees notification/status, staff opens token/QR verification and confirms collection.
Text: "Ready means verifiable, single-use pickup."
Interaction: verify token, confirm collection, show Completed.

### Scene 8 — Manager operations — 1:13-1:25
Visual: manager KPI strip with revenue, completed/total orders, prep time, cancellations, queue and slot metrics.
Text: "Daily operations become visible."
Interaction: reveal sales reports, popular/least ordered items, pickup-slot usage, cancellations, and delayed-order percentage.

### Scene 9 — AI operations cockpit — 1:25-1:30
Visual: dark AI Operations Cockpit with peak window, demand portions, prep plan, waste watchlist, delay risk, sales insight, and recommendations.
Text: "Seven AI signals. One smarter canteen."
Outro: "Smart Canteen — from browse to pickup, every order has a place in the queue."

**Music mood:** upbeat, polished, operational.
**Audio summary:** start with a problem-focused hit, use light clicks for customer actions, build through kitchen and analytics, and resolve with a warm completion tone.
